# A Practical Guide To Molecular Docking  (Backup repo)
*by Elvis A. F.  Martis*

Welcome to the official repository for [**A Practical Guide To Molecular Docking**](https://link.springer.com/book/9783032281661) by Elvis A. F. Martis. This book is a comprehensive guide designed to equip students, researchers, and professionals with the knowledge and tools needed to master molecular docking techniques and their real-world applications in drug discovery. The objective of this textbook is to serve as a handy "user manual" that can explain key steps and points to keep in mind while performing molecular docking.

## Overview

Molecular docking is a key computational technique used in drug design, structural biology, and bioinformatics. By simulating how molecules interact with each other, molecular docking enables the prediction of binding affinities, which play a crucial role in the development of new therapeutic agents and understanding biological processes.

In this book, we explore the underlying principles of molecular docking, discuss various docking software, and provide hands-on examples to help readers better understand how to apply this knowledge to their research.

## Key Features

- **Foundational Principles**: Introduction to the principles of molecular docking, including ligand-receptor interactions, docking algorithms, and scoring functions.
- **Practical Applications**: Insights into how docking is used in drug discovery, virtual screening, and protein-ligand binding studies.
- **Case Studies**: Real-world examples and case studies to illustrate the application of molecular docking in research and industry.
- **Software Tools**: Reviews of popular docking software packages, including AutoDock, Dock, and others, with tutorials on how to use them effectively.
- **Best Practices**: Guidelines on optimising docking simulations, evaluating results, and troubleshooting common issues.
